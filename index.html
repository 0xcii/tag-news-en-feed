Iran’s Revolutionary Guard has been tied to illicit crypto activity for years. In December 2024, U.S. Treasury sanctions targeted wallets linked to IRGC networks moving more than $300 million in stablecoins via an intermediary connected to Yemen’s Houthis. And in June 2025, the pro-Israel hacker group Gonjeshke Darande (“Predatory Sparrow”) stole $90 million from Iranian crypto exchange Nobitex, which Elliptic and others have linked to IRGC activities including ransomware operations. The hack was seen as a hammer blow to Iran, which is alleged to have used crypto to evade sanctions.
