Coinbase also said that the criminals secured government ID images, account balances and corporate data. Two-factor authentication codes and private keys were not breached, it added. The exchange fired staff involved in the breach on the spot and referred to U.S. and international law enforcement. It will also press criminal charges. Coinbase did not immediately respond to CoinDesk's request for comment in regards to ZachXBT's $300 million claim.
