This marks a reversal from the net distribution of more than 200,000 BTC that began in February 2025, coinciding with the onset of bitcoin’s 30% drawdown. With BTC currently flirting with the $97,000 level, this broad-based accumulation indicates a restoration of confidence across investor cohorts.
