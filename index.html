"We believe this collaboration will drive engagement and adoption in ways that were previously difficult to achieve." Soneium went live in January, and at the time, the team said that they hoped to bridge web2 users into the web3 space. The blockchain is a layer-2 on top of Ethereum that uses Optimism’s OP Stack technology. It currently ranks as the 15th largest layer-2 network by total value locked, according to L2 Beat.
