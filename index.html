A phased launch will target sectors with high payment friction first, then expand, RUBx project Dmitry Shumayev reportedly said. The project lands as Moscow tests a separate digital ruble issued by the central bank. The country has been warming up to the cryptocurrency space, with the Bank of Russia earlier this year allowing institutions to offer crypto-linked instruments to qualified investors. Russia’s largest bank Sberbank and Moscow Exchange have already launched products tied to bitcoin (BTC). Cryptocurrencies have also been used to circumvent Western sanctions on Russia’s oil trade.
