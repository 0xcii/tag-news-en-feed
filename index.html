USDe's market cap has risen to nearly $6 billion, while more traditional stablecoins currently have market caps above $50 billion, according to CoinMarketCap data. MEXC’s backing comes as competition in the stablecoin market intensifies, with projects seeking innovative models to ensure stability and accessibility.
