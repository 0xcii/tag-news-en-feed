"If a cyberattack caused the outage of a bank’s own app, but the bank’s backend services were still functioning, customers would still be able to access their accounts with that bank through the ECB’s digital euro app," he said. "Cash is our only true fallback...but as society increasingly moves away from cash, and as cash itself may be difficult to access in emergencies, we need to complement it with a digital version," Cipollone added.
