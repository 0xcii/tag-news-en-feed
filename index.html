The fast-paced growth was spurred by the GENIUS Act, signed into law by U.S. President Donald Trump in July, which set a federal framework for stablecoin issuers and cleared a legal path for financial institutions to adopt the technology. With its stablecoin plan, North Dakota is joining Wyoming as the latest U.S. state experimenting with crypto.
