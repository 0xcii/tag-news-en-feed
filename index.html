Polymarket relies on UMA, an optimistic oracle system, to determine the outcomes of its prediction markets. In this system, anyone can propose a resolution by staking a $750 USDC.e bond, which can then be challenged. If a dispute arises, UMA token holders vote to settle the matter. Polymarket responded via its Discord server, admitting the resolution was unexpected but denying it constituted a "market failure" that would warrant refunds. It clarified that the market was resolved too soon, as no deal between Ukraine and the U.S. has been confirmed, yet it stood by the UMA voting process to correct the outcome.
