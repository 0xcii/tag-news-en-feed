The two companies aim to broaden access to premium investments, such as real estate, according to an emailed announcement on Wednesday. The initiative is also supported by major digital asset custody provider Fireblocks, which provides the multiparty computation wallets that safeguard the tokenized assets on Mavryk's network. The funding round follows the $5.2 million raised by its developers, Mavryk Dynamics, in February. RWAs tokenization refers to representing ownership of real-world assets, such as stocks and bonds, as digital tokens that can be bought and sold on blockchains. Amongst others motivations, one of the proposed advantages of tokenization is generating greater liquidity through expanding access to a larger pool of investors. The idea is that a generally illiquid asset class, such as real estate, could benefit significantly from tokenization. Deloitte has forecast that the global tokenized real estate market could be worth $4 trillion in 10 year's time.
