Clawback refers to tokens that have a feature allowing the issuer to reclaim or "claw back" these tokens from users' wallets under certain conditions. The Friday update will improve the regulatory compliance of XRP Ledger’s Automated Market Maker (AMM) pools, allowing tokens with the clawback enabled to be used.
