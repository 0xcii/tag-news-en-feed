Any commissioner can ask that an SEC action be reviewed. In the past, commissioners have asked to review ETF disapprovals, for example. The letter did not indicate which commissioner or commissioners asked for the review. GDLC is benchmarked to CoinDesk's CoinDesk 5 Index.
