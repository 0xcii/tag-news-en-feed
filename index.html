Poor user experience and onboarding issues were identified as the industry’s biggest hurdles by 54% of respondents, down from 80% in 2023, reflecting efforts to simplify blockchain gaming and make it more accessible. “Innovations in design and functionality have reduced barriers and these advancements are helping to ensure that the Web3 gaming ecosystem is more intuitive, inclusive, and welcoming to a broader audience,” said Sebastien Borget, president of the BGA and co-founder and COO of The Sandbox.
