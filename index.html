EigenLayer’s ecosystem has been expanding, with "100+" AVSs in development, according to its website. Notable services include EigenDA, a data availability service operated by Eigen Labs, and ARPA Network, which specializes in trustless randomization. While EigenLayer pioneered restaking, the lack of slashing left room for competitors to gain market share.
