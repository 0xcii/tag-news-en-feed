Traditional finance often struggles with DvP transactions, which require payments to be made before or at the same time as delivery of securities, due to fragmented systems and manual steps that delay settlement, the release notes. It points to data suggesting payment and settlement failures have cost market participants over $900 billion in the past 10 years.
